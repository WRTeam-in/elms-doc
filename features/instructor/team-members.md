# Team Members

![Team Members](../../static/images/web/team_member.png)

The Team Members section is available to team instructors and displays all co-instructors who have been invited to join the team. New members can be invited by entering the email address of an active instructor account. Existing team members are listed below and can be removed at any time. All course earnings are credited to the course owner (the team instructor); manual splits can be arranged separately if needed.
