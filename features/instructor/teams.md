# Teams

The Teams section appears for instructors who are co-instructors of one or more team accounts. It lists all the teams they have joined, and clicking any team opens that team's dedicated view.

The team view includes a sidebar with an option to return to individual instructor mode, along with team-specific sections for Courses, Assignments, Reviews, Quiz Reports, and Notifications. These sections work the same as their counterparts in the main instructor panel, with the distinction that co-instructors can only access and manage courses they have been added to within that team.
